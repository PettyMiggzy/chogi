How to read it

  • Bigger bubble = bigger bag. The top 250 holders by supply share.
  • Connected lines = same hands. Bubblemaps groups wallets that have transferred to each other — those clusters are usually the same person or coordinated group.
  • Clusters near each other = suspicious. Top 5 wallets all linked? That's a single whale with 5 wallets, not 5 independent holders.
  • Hover to see addresses, balances, and labels (CEX wallets, contracts, etc).
